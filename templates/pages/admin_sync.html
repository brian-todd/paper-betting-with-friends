Background Jobs

The *-seed jobs run only when you ask. A seed loads venues, teams and the calendar as well as games and lines — the periodic jobs cover games and lines only, and skip any game whose teams are not already stored. Run a seed once per season, or whenever a sport shows no games at all. Leave the season blank to load the current one, or set it to backfill an earlier year.
